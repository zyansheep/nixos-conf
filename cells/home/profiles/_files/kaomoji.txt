# Kaomoji for emoji-picker (see ../emoji.nix), hand-made and hand-picked.
#
# `## emoji mood · keywords` starts a group. Each line under it is one
# kaomoji, optionally followed by two or more spaces and its own extra
# keywords. The picker shows and searches `kaomoji  mood · group keywords,
# own keywords`, and lists the group right after its emoji, so kaomoji sit
# among the emoji they're like instead of after all ~3.9k emoji. That
# holds in search too: Vicinae gives most matches the same score and keeps
# list order among equals. Several groups may share an emoji (listed in
# file order); one that isn't in the emoji list fails the build.
# A kaomoji may appear only once and may not contain two spaces in a row
# (the build fails otherwise). Lines starting with `# ` are comments.
#
# Drawn for how they render here: Vicinae has no font of its own, so it's
# DejaVu Sans with Noto/IPA fallbacks. In DejaVu the modifier letters
# (ᵔ ᵕ ˃ ˂ ⁰) shrink to specks, so eyes and mouths use full-size glyphs
# (^ > < ᴗ ω ▽ ᗜ), and stacked combining marks are avoided.
